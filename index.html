Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a lens to my collection?

Tap the "+" button in the main view. You can either browse the database of 320+ pre-loaded lenses (tap "Browse Common Lenses"), or manually enter all lens details. When browsing, filter by brand and mount to quickly find your lens.

How do I browse the lens database?

When adding a lens, tap "Browse Common Lenses". Use the brand and mount filters at the top to narrow down results. The database includes 320+ lenses from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Sigma, Tamron, and other major brands. Tap any lens to auto-fill its specifications.

How do I edit a lens?

Tap on any lens in your collection to view its details, then tap the edit button (pencil icon) in the top right. You can modify any information including specifications, purchase details, ratings, and notes.

How do I delete a lens?

In the main list view, swipe left on any lens and tap "Delete". You'll be asked to confirm the deletion. Alternatively, you can mark a lens as "Sold" to keep its records while removing it from active inventory.

How do I rate my lenses?

When adding or editing a lens, scroll to the "Purpose & Rating" section. Tap the stars to rate from 1–5, or tap a filled star to remove the rating. You can also sort your collection by rating to see your favorites first.

How does the coverage analysis work? PRO

Tap the chart icon in the toolbar to view your collection's coverage. The analysis shows: 1) Focal length coverage across your collection, 2) Aperture range visualization, 3) Focal length vs aperture scatter plot, and 4) Gap analysis identifying missing focal lengths or aperture ranges.

How do I track warranties? PRO

When adding or editing a lens, scroll to "Insurance (Optional)" and tap "Add Warranty Expiry Date". Enter the warranty end date and optionally add warranty details. Lenses expiring within 30 days will trigger alerts on the main screen.

How do I view my collection's total value? PRO

Tap the dollar sign icon ($) in the toolbar to view the Gear Value screen. This shows total purchase value and insured value by currency, plus a sortable list of all lenses from most to least expensive.

How do I export my collection? PRO

Go to the Import/Export screen (tap the up arrow icon). Tap "Export to CSV" to create a backup file of your entire collection including all specifications, ratings, purchase info, warranty data, and notes.

How do I import lenses from CSV? PRO

Go to Import/Export and tap "Import from CSV". Select your CSV file. The importer automatically detects duplicates and skips them. After import, you'll see a summary of total records, successfully imported, and skipped duplicates.

How does the Lending Tracker work? PRO

When editing a lens, record who you lent it to and the date. A dedicated "Lent Out" screen shows every lens currently borrowed so you always know who has your gear. Tap a lens there to mark it as returned.

What are Condition Details? PRO

You can flag a lens as having fungus, mark whether you still have the original box and packaging, and track its functional status. Visual indicators appear throughout your collection so problem lenses are easy to spot at a glance.

How does the Wishlist work? PRO

The Wishlist lets you add lenses you plan to buy, set a target price, and assign a priority (High / Medium / Low). Sort your wishlist by priority, date added, name, or price. Once you buy a lens, mark it as purchased to move it into your inventory.

What are Gear Kits? PRO

Gear Kits let you group lenses into named sets for different shooting scenarios — for example a "Wedding" kit, a "Travel" kit, or an "Astro" kit. The kit view shows which lenses are available and which are currently lent out, so you can pack smarter before every shoot.

How do I filter and sort my collection?

Use the filter bar at the bottom of the main screen (iPhone) or left sidebar (iPad). You can: 1) Sort by brand, rating, focal length, aperture, weight, purchase date, or release year, 2) Filter by lens type (Prime/Zoom), brand, mount, 3) Show only weather-sealed or stabilized lenses, 4) Include/exclude sold lenses.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. All your data is stored locally on your device. GearGrove doesn't sync to any cloud service, which means your lens collection data stays completely private and secure. You control your data through manual CSV exports (Pro feature).

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use the Browse database feature to quickly add lenses with accurate specifications
  • Add serial numbers to help with insurance claims and theft recovery
  • Rate your lenses to identify favorites and underused gear
  • Use Gear Kits to pre-plan what to bring for each type of shoot
  • Add lenses to the Wishlist to track what you want next and compare target prices
  • Use the Lending Tracker whenever you lend a lens — so you never forget who has it
  • Consider upgrading to Pro for warranty tracking, lending, kits, and collection insights
  • Mark lenses as sold rather than deleting to maintain history

Política de Privacidad

Fecha de vigencia: 7 de noviembre de 2025

Resumen general

GearGrove se compromete a proteger tu privacidad. Esta Política de Privacidad explica cómo manejamos tu información cuando usas nuestra aplicación de gestión de inventario de objetivos fotográficos.

Recopilación y almacenamiento de datos

Solo almacenamiento local: GearGrove almacena todos los datos únicamente en tu dispositivo. No se transmiten datos personales a servidores ni a terceros.

La siguiente información se almacena localmente en tu dispositivo:

  • Especificaciones del objetivo (marca, modelo, longitud focal, apertura, etc.)
  • Información de compra (fecha, precio, moneda, estado)
  • Calificaciones personales y notas
  • Datos de seguimiento de uso
  • Información de seguro y garantía (función Pro)
  • Números de serie y versiones de firmware
  • Registros de préstamos (función Pro)
  • Lista de deseos y kits de equipo (función Pro)
  • Cualquier otro dato que ingreses en la aplicación

Compras dentro de la app

GearGrove ofrece una compra única opcional (GearGrove Pro) procesada a través del sistema de compras dentro de la app de Apple. No recopilamos ni almacenamos información de pago. Todo el procesamiento de pagos es manejado de forma segura por Apple.

No se requiere cuenta

No es necesario crear una cuenta ni iniciar sesión. Tus datos permanecen completamente privados y bajo tu control.

No se requiere conexión a Internet

La aplicación funciona totalmente sin conexión, excepto durante el procesamiento de compras dentro de la app a través del sistema de pago seguro de Apple.

Compartición de datos

No compartimos, vendemos ni alquilamos tu información a terceros. Todos los datos permanecen locales en tu dispositivo y no tenemos acceso a ellos.

Exportación de datos

Con GearGrove Pro, puedes exportar tu colección en formato CSV en cualquier momento. Tú controlas dónde se guarda el archivo y quién tiene acceso.

Análisis y seguimiento

GearGrove no utiliza ningún tipo de servicio de análisis, herramientas de seguimiento ni redes publicitarias.

Servicios de terceros

No se integran servicios de terceros, con excepción del sistema de compras dentro de la app de Apple.

Seguridad de los datos

Dado que todos los datos se almacenan localmente en tu dispositivo, su seguridad depende de las medidas de seguridad de tu dispositivo (código de acceso, Face ID, Touch ID, etc.).

Eliminación de datos

Puedes eliminar todos tus datos en cualquier momento desinstalando la app. El historial de compras dentro de la app permanece vinculado a tu Apple ID y puede restaurarse si vuelves a instalar la aplicación.
